# Material parameter identifiers

**Status (measured):** **52 of 324** distinct material parameter identifiers are named (**16.05 percent**); 272 remain unnamed. A name here is a **hash-preimage match** under Jenkins one-at-a-time over a normalized string, **not a confirmed original spelling** (inferred). Only hashes, candidate strings and aggregate layout numbers appear below.

Grades used throughout: **measured** = counted or hash-checked in the local record; **inferred** = interpretation of measured evidence; **external** = from outside the local record. No claim in this document is external. The table grades hash and layout separately from the spelling.

## Named identifiers

Kinds are observed, not predicted. A texture carries a slot; a constant buffer (cb) carries a buffer number and byte offsets. Offsets are byte ranges aggregated over every observed material record in the local metadata; a single value means the identifier was seen at only one offset in that buffer. Ranges can span different material classes, so they are layout observations, not semantic assignments (measured).

## Method

- **Hash.** Jenkins one-at-a-time (`joaat`) over the lowercased, flat-concatenated name: no separators, no case variants, no prefix. The same spelling is hashed with an independent wide (`u64`, explicitly masked to 32 bits) accumulator (measured).
- **Control vector.** `joaat("diffusetex") = 0xc8e8c282` passed before and after every scan; the wide accumulator reproduced it, mixed-case folding passed, and the three further controls `speculartex2` = `739e3d0d`, `tintpalettetex` = `2c8d7182` and `dirtdiffusetex` = `7485dafb` reproduced (measured). An earlier round ran a four-preimage control set at 4/4 with the same mixed-case check (measured).
- **Grammar discovered.** The productive form is flat lowercase concatenation: feature + channel + property, and feature + resource noun (inferred from matches). Confirmed endings include `texture`, `map`, `tex`, `uvset`, `intensity`, `blend`, `scale`, `factor`, `multiplier`, `bias`, `softness`, `roughness`, `opacity`, `min`, `max`, `amount` and `offset`; numbered endings `diffusetex2` and `speculartex2` also occur (measured). Internal tokens seen in matches include `diffuse`, `bump`, `palette`, `mask`, `height`, `blend`, `tint`, `uv`, `lookup`, `dirt`, `dust`, `tessellation`, `control` and `grass` (measured).
- **Yield by stage.** 26 from general wordlists and prior work, 17 from the inferred compound grammar, 5 from targeted stems and numbered endings, 1 from class-segmented vocabularies, 3 from in-band token vocabulary; total 52 (measured). The class-segmented hit came from shared vocabulary, not an exclusive segment (measured).
- **Chance checks.** At the in-band stage, 794,713 fresh candidates gave an expected 0.0509 accidental matches against the then-unnamed 275 identifiers and produced 3 new hits; the uniform-baseline Poisson tail is about 2.11e-5 (measured count, inferred model). That is aggregate support, not proof of any one spelling (inferred).
- **No external claims.** Every number above comes from local runs or local records (measured).

### Categories that produced zero

- Underscore suffixes and `ph_` or other prefixes: **0** across **37,512,712** candidates (28,244,208 + 9,268,504) (measured).
- Generic qualifier expansion: **0** across **855,000** candidates (measured, as recorded).
- Component properties (`r`, `g`, `b`, `a`, `rgb`, `rgba`, `alpha`, `luminance`): **0** across **156,490** candidates (144,427 + 12,063) (measured).
- Indexed properties: **0** across **105,632** candidates; explicit `layerN` forms: **0** across **6,720**; paired bounds: **0** across **8,470** (measured).
- Class-exclusive vocabularies: ground-only and shell-only cohorts produced **0 directed hits** across **574,573** distinct strings; structural-only also produced 0 (measured). The single class-pass hit came from shared vocabulary (measured).
- Large flat-concatenation sweep: **495,465,399** targeted candidates against the 10 unnamed ground identifiers produced **0 promoted names**; 3 apparent hits matched a chance expectation of 1.50 (Poisson tail 0.191) (measured count, inferred verdict).
- Numbered rider families found only the already-known `diffusetex2` and `speculartex2`; **0 new** numbered identifiers (measured).
